Why Inkguard

🖥️ On Your Computer

All processing happens locally. Your files never leave your computer, never reach the internet, and we never receive them.

🔐 Air-Gapped

No network calls, no telemetry, no phone-home. Designed for offline use. You can turn off the internet while it works.

📋 Evidence Report

Every batch produces a dated, signed evidence report documenting what was removed, who reviewed it, and measured accuracy for that version.

👥 Multi-Reviewer

Built for two independent reviewers plus physician sign-off. Review state, marked words, and disagreements are all documented.

✓ Transparent

Open-source. Measured against published benchmarks. See exactly what it removes and why.

Processes Word docs, PDFs, spreadsheets, CSV files, and more—including hidden content, metadata, tracked changes, and comments.

Verify Integrity

Every release is signed with SHA-256 checksums. After downloading, verify that your copy matches the checksum published on the GitHub Releases page.

On macOS or Linux:

shasum -a 256 inkguard-1.0.0.dmg
# Compare the output with the value on the release page

On Windows (PowerShell):

(Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 inkguard-1.0.0.exe).Hash
# Compare the output with the value on the release page

What This Software Is — and Isn't

✓ Safe Harbor De-Identification

Inkguard assists with the Safe Harbor method (45 CFR 164.514(b)(2)). It removes the identifiers the method lists, documents every step, and marks uncertain items for human review. The final de-identification determination is made by your authorized reviewers and physician, not by the software alone.

✗ Not HIPAA Certified

We do not claim HIPAA compliance or certification. Inkguard is a tool that documents your due diligence. HIPAA compliance is your responsibility as a covered entity. We recommend you consult counsel before using de-identified data.

✓ Local Processing, No Network

Files are processed entirely on your computer. We never receive your files, run no analytics, collect no telemetry, and have no network code. Nothing leaves your computer unless you choose to send a feedback report.

✓ Open Source & Transparent

Inkguard is open-source (Apache 2.0). You can read the code, audit it, and build it yourself. Measured accuracy is published with test set details. No black boxes.
